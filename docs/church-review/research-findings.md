## New entrants (prayer-app / ChMS-adjacent segment)

None found that materially change the competitive set. Details:

- **Instapray** — defunct. Sold to Salem Media Group; subsequently shut down. Not an active competitor. The domain `instapray.app` still resolves but the prior app is discontinued. Exclude.
- **YouVersion (Bible App) "Prayer List" + "Prayer Chain"** — free, bundled inside the Bible App for 24,000+ churches. Not priced, not a SaaS competitor. Relevant as a **free-surface anchor** pastors may cite ("we already do prayer through YouVersion"). Not new in April 2026 — has existed for years; included here because the task description named it.
- **Nucleus (nucleus.church)** — **worth watching.** Bundles a "PrayerFlow" private prayer hub inside a $129/mo (Web+Sermons) or $199/mo (full suite) ChMS offering. Not a direct prayer-first competitor — they are in Subsplash/Pushpay territory — but prayer is a named feature. If their PrayerFlow feature-set expands or they start selling it standalone, re-rank them in the next delta refresh.
- No other net-new prayer-first SaaS products surfaced in web search for "prayer app church launch 2026" or adjacent queries.

## Integration landscape — competitors' ChMS integrations

Context: PrayerJar shipped **Planning Center** as its first ChMS integration in Sprint 18 (2026-04-18). The mirror question: who else offers ChMS integrations, and does anyone in the direct prayer-first segment match us?

| Competitor | Has ChMS integration? | Which ChMS |
|---|---|---|
| Prayer Platform | **No** | — |
| PrayerMate | **No** | Social media only |
| Echo Prayer | **No** | — |
| Uplift Prayer | **No** | — |
| Subsplash | Yes (Enterprise only) | Rock RMS |
| Pushpay | Yes | 80+ integrations (ChMS ecosystem broadly) |
| Tithe.ly | Yes | Elvanto, Planning Center, Ministry Platform, Shelby v5/Arena, CCB, Breeze, Rock RMS, F1, Faith Teams |
| Planning Center | N/A (is a ChMS) | — |
| Breeze | Is a ChMS; Tithely ecosystem integrations | — |

**Decisive finding:** Among the four direct prayer-first competitors (Prayer Platform, PrayerMate, Echo, Uplift), **none has any ChMS integration** — still. PrayerJar's PCO integration **remains a category-unique differentiator two days after Sprint 18 shipped.** This is still worth the pricing-page and sales real-estate we committed to it.

## What to flag for Strategist

1. **Hold the ladder.** One competitor moved — Breeze — in a tier not adjacent to ours. No justification to revisit the 2026-04-17 Free/$19/$49/$199+ decision.
2. **Reinforce the PCO story.** Two days after ship, we are still alone among direct prayer-first competitors in having any ChMS integration. The pricing-page "Planning Center integration included" bullet on Growing Church ($49) is carrying more weight than the April 17 report could foresee — that report anticipated this but now the "no one else has this" claim is time-dated and provable.
3. **Data quality caveats.** (a) Prayer Platform's pricing page still would not yield a price via WebFetch — the $10 entry remains third-party-sourced. (b) Planning Center's pricing uses a quote-builder; the tier ladder is not on the public page. Both were true on April 17 and are true today.
